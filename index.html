What we are

A network of ordinary Nigerians documenting the roads their government won't.

The Road Reporters is an initiative of Community Agenda for Peace. We recruit and train everyday citizens, commercial drivers, students, traders, and residents, to document road conditions across Nigeria with photos, locations, and short reports.

Every submission builds a public case file. Not complaints that disappear into an inbox, but evidence: dated, located, and impossible to ignore. When enough voices point at the same failure, it becomes harder to look away from.

Member's commitment

What every Road Reporter agrees to

Accurate reporting is what gives this network its power. Before you register, this is the standard every member holds themselves to.

"I agree to report road conditions truthfully and responsibly, provide accurate information, and support efforts to promote safer and better roads in Nigeria."

Signing this doesn't register you. Register separately with the membership form above, then bring your passport photo to our office to collect your free ID.
